Questions

Good to know.

What pets do you groom?

Dogs, cats, birds and small pets.

How much will it cost?

Prices start from what’s on the menu and depend on your pet’s size and coat. We confirm the final price on WhatsApp before your visit, so there are no surprises.

Do you come to my home?

Yes — our grooming van comes to your door. Choose “At your home” when you book and tell us where you are.

How long does a groom take?

From 30 minutes to 3 hours, depending on the service and your pet’s size and coat.

How do I book?

Choose where, what and when above, and WhatsApp opens with your booking ready. Press send and we confirm your time in seconds, any time of day.

My pet gets nervous. Is that okay?

Of course. We work calmly, never rush, and give every pet the time they need. Mention it in the notes when you book.
