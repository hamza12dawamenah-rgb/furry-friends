Questions

Good to know.

What pets do you groom?

Dogs, cats, birds and rodents.

Do you come to my home?

Yes — we offer mobile grooming at your doorstep. Tap “Book a home visit” and tell us where you are.

How long does a groom take?

From 30 minutes to 3 hours, depending on the service and your pet’s size and coat.

How do I book?

Tap any “Book” button — WhatsApp opens with your message ready. We reply in seconds, any time of day.

Can you help with grooming at home between visits?

Of course. We’re happy to advise on a routine at home that makes grooming sessions easier for you and your pet.
